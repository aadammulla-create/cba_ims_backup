Student Reservation Process Overview

How instructional equipment is requested, claimed at the custodian counter, and returned.

Start a Request
STEP 01

Select Equipment

Browse available classroom equipment categories and confirm the date, time slot, and room number for your academic activity.

STEP 02

Submit & Save Token

Complete the reservation form with your Student ID and course details, then save your reservation lookup token.

STEP 03

Counter Claim & ID

Check approval via Track Reservation, then present your lookup token and deposit your physical School ID within 15 minutes of start time.

STEP 04

Return & Clearance

Return all borrowed items to the CBA Custodian Counter on schedule for condition verification and immediate return of your School ID.
